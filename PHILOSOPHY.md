We believe in security, privacy, and good code. At ApexOS, we aim to differenciate from other ublueos-based projects by being only a layer upon a Fedora base to give the user the most control about their experience. We aim to accept contributions from everyone, stay respectful of each other and set the politics aside to all be tech nerds again for a moment while working on this project's code. We do not believe excluding or censoring anyone is worth it.

Why build on top of fedora on an already crowded space of ublueos-clones? Because of a lack of available projects that are user-centric and do not impose their choices on users while still being usable, optimal and trustworthy out of the box. We believe in mindful defaults, customisable all at first boot. Overkill security, that's why images are cryptograhically signed using the cosign standard and automatically verified at each update. You choose what apps you install, what setings are changed, all in a secure and usable by default operating system. If these goals resonate with you, you've come to the right place.

First of all, to install ApexOS, you will need an empty SSD of which the content will be erased to install ApexOS. Then, you need an empty USB drive of at least 8Gb of which the content will be erased to install ApexOS's live installer. That USB key will be usable for anything else again after ApexOS is installed. Next, download a tool called [rufus](https://rufus.ie/en/) if you are currently on windows, or [Impression](https://flathub.org/apps/io.gitlab.adhami3310.Impression) if you are on linux. Then, you need to download the ApexOS system image here. Connect the USB key to your PC, open the tool you chose and select the apexos system image. Then, select the USB key and start the process without touching anything other setting.
Once that process is finished, power down your computer and plug the SSD in. For a problem-free install, we recommend unplugging any other internal drive before installing ApexOS to avoid any mistakes on your part. Then, you need to hold a key to access the BIOS of your computer immediately after you press the power button. This key is usually F11, F12, delete, F1, F2, F9, or another F key, consult your manufacturer's manual to know how to proceed into the BIOS boot selection menu. You might need to find and disable an option called "Secure Boot" in your BIOS, which is a proprietary microsoft technology design to stop Linux usage. Then, select your USB key fro the BIOS boot menu using the arrow keys on your keyboard and press enter to boot into ApexOS's installer.

Then, explore the options on screen to install the operating system. First off, you need to setup your wifi connecrtion if you have one. If your PC is connected to internet via ethernet, it should be automatically recognized and there is no need to configure it.

Now, configure a user account by clicking on the user menu. The root menu does not need to be open unless you know what you are doing. Enter your favorite username and password, and click Done. You might need to click Done two times if your password is considered very weak. In that case, it is highly recommended to change it.
